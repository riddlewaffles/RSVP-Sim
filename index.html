What is RSVP?

RSVP is a speed-reading technique where words appear one at a time at a fixed point on the screen, usually the center. This eliminates the need for eye movements across lines of text, which are a major bottleneck in traditional reading. By removing mechanical overhead like regressions (re-reading) and subvocalization (saying words in your head), RSVP allows your brain to process text at higher speeds, often 400–600 words per minute or more, depending on practice and familiarity with the material.
